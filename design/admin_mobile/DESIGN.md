## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an evocative, high-stakes visual identity engineered for elite nighttime theatrical productions, sensual pole performance events, VIP table reservations, and QR access control. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Dark Glamour & Theatrical Noir**—a fusion of cabaret mystique, sultry stage lighting, and contemporary luxury nightlife. It balances seductive, dramatic stage presence with uncompromising functional clarity for fast-paced event check-in, real-time ticket scanning, and guest tier management in low-light environments.

### Core Attributes
- **Mysterious & Sensual:** Deep emerald and charcoal tones, diffused backlights, and smoky silhouettes create an intoxicating aura of VIP exclusivity.
- **Opulent & Prestigious:** Warm copper-gold filigree lines, satin finishes, and sharp typographic hierarchy evoke vintage burlesque elevated to ultra-modern boutique hospitality.
- **Operational Precision:** High legibility under strobe and club lighting conditions, intuitive QR code validation states, and effortless flow for venue staff and affluent patrons.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for pitch-black and stage-lit environments, minimizing harsh white glare while maximizing contrast and sensual warmth.

### Functional Palette Structure
- **Canvas Base (`#717973`):** Deep obsidian abyss ensuring maximum depth and seamless blending with mobile OLED displays.
- **Surface Elevation (`#151314`):** Smoked dark surface for container cards, navigation bars, and scanning viewfinders.
- **Primary Accent (`#03D29A`):** Vibrant neon stage emerald used for primary calls to action, active states, VIP status badges, and scanning reticles.
- **Dramatic Amber (`#FFB659`):** Deep theatrical tone used for secondary interactive states, table category badges, and active sheet headers.
- **Warm Muted Accent (`#4B826A`):** Subtle champagne copper used for delicate 1px luxury borders, crown badges, VIP seat reservations, and curated editorial emphasis.
- **Primary Typography (`#E8E1E2`):** Warm ivory silk that provides comfortable, glare-free readability against pitch-black backgrounds.
- **Subtle Muted Text (`#E1BEBE`):** Desaturated taupe for metadata, timestamps, table numbers, and secondary form labels.

### Atmospheric Gradients & Glows
- **Rim-Light Spotlight:** Radial wash (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(3, 210, 154, 0.28) 0%, rgba(11, 9, 10, 0) 75%)`) layered beneath top navigation and stage detail hero headers.
- **Gold Foil Border:** Linear gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(75, 130, 106, 0.6) 0%, rgba(255, 182, 89, 0.2) 100%)`) applied to VIP cards and modal dividers.

## Typography

The typographic balance pairs theatrical, high-contrast serif headlines with pristine, ultra-legible geometric modern sans-serif body and operational data.

- **Display & Editorial Subtitles (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes luxury performance programs, vintage playbills, and exclusive nightclub invitations. Used for performer names, event headings, tier titles, and celebratory check-in notifications.
- **UI, Form Inputs & Scanning Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides razor-sharp legibility at small sizes, ensuring QR codes, attendee names, document IDs (DNI / Pasaporte), guest lists, and door admission counts are instantly readable without fatigue.
- **Stylistic Display Treatments:** Event logos and marquee hero titles may incorporate dramatic uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.12em`) or custom brush-script accents (for artist signatures), paired with soft warm gold or neon outer text glow (`text-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(3, 210, 154, 0.6)`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture employs a structured fluid grid optimized for high-pressure door host operations on mobile handhelds and rich backstage administration dashboards on tablet and desktop.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px):** Single-column stacked fluid grid with `16px (1rem)` margins and gutters. Optimized for rapid single-handed thumb interaction when scanning QR badges or validating guest identities.
- **Tablet Viewport (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid with `24px (1.5rem)` gutters and margins. Ideal for reception stands, live table seating maps, and box office ticket verification.
- **Desktop Viewport (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max system (`1280px` max-width container) centered with `3.5rem` outer margins. Provides high-density overview grids for attendee capacities, performer line-ups, and beverage minimum tracking.

### Rhythm & Density
The design utilizes a strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm. Fast-interaction surfaces (scanner interfaces, badge status tallies) favor compact padding (`space-xs` and `space-sm`), while promotional guest interfaces and VIP seating showcases leverage generous `space-lg` and `space-xl` breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is produced using dark atmospheric tiers, stage-inspired rim lights, and soft metallic perimeter strokes rather than traditional daylight drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#717973` dark ground.
- **Level 1 (Card Containers & Lists):** `#151314` surface finished with a crisp, low-opacity border `1px solid rgba(75, 130, 106, 0.15)`.
- **Level 2 (Active VIP Cards & Scanner HUDs):** `#211F20` surface with emerald back-glow `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(3, 210, 154, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Bottom Sheets & Validation Overlays):** `#100E0F` surface wrapped in gold framing `1px solid rgba(75, 130, 106, 0.45)` with ambient backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).

### Lighting & Rim Effects
To mimic stage lighting, featured performer cards and hero VIP sections receive an inner specular highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(232, 225, 226, 0.15)`), giving UI surfaces a polished obsidian or lacquered mahogany presence.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a sophisticated **Rounded (`2`)** geometry (`8px / 0.5rem` baseline, `16px / 1rem` for medium containers, and `24px / 1.5rem` for prominent dialog cards).

- **Standard Buttons & Inputs:** `8px` corner radius providing crisp architectural precision without looking blunt or aggressive.
- **VIP Cards, QR Containers & Modals:** `16px` to `24px` radius to reflect smooth velvet banquettes and plush cabaret club interiors.
- **Pill Badges (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for attendee status indicators (*"VIP Platino"*, *"Ingreso Permitido"*, *"Mesa Reservada"*), creating instant visual contrast against rectangular containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Golden Emerald):** Gradient background from `#03D29A` to `#028C67` with warm ivory text (`#E8E1E2`), crisp `8px` radius, subtle gold rim hover effect, and glowing shadow. Used for *"Validar Entrada"*, *"Confirmar Asistencia"*, or *"Reservar Mesa"*.
- **Secondary (Burlesque Gold Outline):** Transparent background, `1px solid #4B826A`, warm gold text with subtle ivory hover fill. Used for filtering ticket tiers or downloading attendee manifests.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border, muted taupe text (`#E1BEBE`) shifting to gold on focus. Used for cancel actions and secondary navigation.

### QR Code Scanning Viewfinder
- High-contrast camera viewport surrounded by dark smoked translucent overlays (`rgba(11, 9, 10, 0.8)`).
- Viewfinder corners highlighted with 3px warm gold brackets (`#4B826A`).
- Dynamic center laser scan line featuring emerald illumination (`#03D29A`) with a vertical ease-in-out sweep animation.
- Instant validation state: Reticle transitions to vibrant emerald for *"Ingreso Válido"* or warning tone with pulse for *"Entrada Duplicada / No Válida"*.

### VIP & General Admission Cards
- Deep surface (`#151314`) with hairline gold border (`rgba(75, 130, 106, 0.2)`).
- Contains attendee tier pill badge, perforated ticket notch graphic on left and right borders, and high-contrast monochrome QR payload area framed in warm ivory.
- Bottom metadata includes assigned table (*"Mesa VIP 04"*), door host confirmation timestamp, and guest list status.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Dark lacquered field fill (`#100E0F`) with `1px solid rgba(75, 130, 106, 0.2)` border.
- Placeholder text in subdued taupe (`#E1BEBE`).
- Focus state activates an intense emerald outline (`#03D29A`) accompanied by an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(3, 210, 154, 0.2)` ring.
- Mobile check-in quick-search includes clear-button actions and integrated barcode toggle for fast manual DNI lookups.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with uppercase typography (`label-sm`).
- **Check-in Confirmado:** Emerald glow on dark forest base (`rgba(3, 210, 154, 0.15)` border & text).
- **VIP Gold Access:** Radiant copper-gold base (`#FFB659`) with midnight black text (`#0B090A`).
- **General Access:** Border with `#E8E1E2` text.

### Attendance Check-in Lists & Data Tables
- Flush dark rows separated by razor-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(75, 130, 106, 0.08)`).
- Alternating row interaction highlights on touch/hover (`rgba(3, 210, 154, 0.08)`).
- Left-aligned guest details with prominent avatar or VIP tier initial; right-aligned instantaneous action trigger (one-tap scan verification toggle).
